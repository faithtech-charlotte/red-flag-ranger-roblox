# Red Flag Rangers

A Roblox pet adventure (ages 9 to 13) built for the FaithTech hackathon with Present Age Ministries. Players adopt a dog, play, earn Paw Coins, dress up their pup, and meet NPCs. One NPC's story gently practices recognizing and responding to online grooming warning signs.

> All educational dialogue is **provisional** until Present Age reviews it. It lives in `src/server/Content/Encounters.luau`.

## Run it

1. Install [Rojo](https://rojo.space) 7.7.1 (`brew install rojo` or `rokit add rojo-rbx/rojo@7.7.1`).
2. Pick one:
   - **Build a place file:** `rojo build -o "red-flag-ranger-roblox.rbxlx"`, then open it in Roblox Studio.
   - **Live sync:** `rojo serve`, open any place in Studio, then Plugins → Rojo → Connect.
3. Press **Play**. The world, NPCs and UI are all generated by code at runtime, so Edit mode shows only the baseplate.

**Saving progress:** DataStores only work in a **published** place with *Game Settings → Security → Enable Studio Access to API Services* turned on. Without that, the game still runs on a temporary profile and shows a "Not saving" chip.

**Tests:** the pure logic (encounter engine, purchases, profile migrations, conditions, catalogs) has unit tests in `tests/`. In Studio's command bar: `print(require(game.ServerStorage.Tests)())`

## Phase 2: your dog as a companion

| Feature | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Pet menu** | Press **Q** (gamepad **Y**) near your dog, tap its prompt, or press **🐶 My Dog**. Shows name, Bond Level, next unlock, and only the actions that fit right now. Locked or cooling-down actions show why. While it's open, your dog sits and pays attention. |
| **Actions** | Pet, Feed (free kibble every 45 s, or treats), Fetch, Brush, Sit, Stay (Bond Level 2), Come, Tricks (Spin at 3, High-five at 5, Play Bow at 6, Zoomies at 8). |
| **Bond XP** | Earned from petting, feeding, fetch, brushing, tricks, the spa, training, and activities. Per-source cooldowns plus a soft daily cap (full XP, then half, then 0). Bond never goes down. Level 10 gives the Best Friend Collar. All numbers are in `src/shared/Catalogs/BondCatalog.luau`. |
| **Fetch** | Aim and throw: point and click, tap the ground, or aim with the camera (gamepad RT). Works anywhere. 3 catches in Sunny Dog Park complete the park challenge. Balls that land on roofs or out of bounds "poof" and you get a new one. |
| **Frisbee Catch** | Frisbee Field, east of the Training Grounds. "Grab a frisbee" at the stack by the throwing line: your dog runs out and waits. Hold to wind up, release to throw; aim up for a higher, floatier throw. The disc flies with real disc aerodynamics (lift, drag, and the turn-and-fade of a spinning disc; `src/shared/FrisbeeMath.luau`). The dog sprints to where the disc will come down and leaps for it. Only mid-air catches count; it still brings back the ones that land, then runs out again. 3 catches complete the game (same coins as fetch). Walking away, the whistle, or Stop ends it. |
| **Spa** | Grooming tubs at the salon: brush, wash, rinse, dry. Tap or sweep over the spots. Your dog sparkles afterward. |
| **Trick training** | Training mat at the Training Grounds. Press when the marker is in the green zone; the server does the timing. Gets harder each time you finish. |
| **Agility** | Same course, now with one-time Gold (<16 s) and Silver (<24 s) bonuses. |
| **New places** | Bo's Treat Shop (treats), Ranger HQ (mission board and campfire; Ranger Lee is still there), ball bucket and squeaky toys in the park, signposts at the crossroads. |

**Behavior states** (`src/server/Logic/PetStateMachine.luau`): Following, Sitting, Staying (base); Idle, Eating, BeingPetted, Grooming, PerformingTrick (temporary; they return to the base state on their own); MovingToTarget, Fetching, Returning (driven by an activity). One `AlignPosition` moves the dog, and each state says where it points, so movers never compete.

**Activities** (`src/server/Content/Activities.luau` + `src/server/Activities/*`): definitions hold rewards and rules; mechanics do the playing. `ActivityService.complete()` is the only place that pays rewards.

**Animations** are procedural (no uploaded assets). To use real animations, put asset ids in `PetCatalog.AnimationIds` keyed by state; the procedural pose is the fallback.

## Playing with your dog (feel overhaul)

- **Whistle:** always on screen once you have a dog. H / gamepad D-pad Up / the 📣 button (above the jump button on touch screens) calls your dog back from anywhere: a chirp, a "!" over the dog, and it drops whatever it was doing and runs to you.
- **Action bar** (bottom-left) slides in when you stand still near your dog and hides while you play. Q / gamepad Y open it from anywhere; on touch, use the 🐶 button.
- **Petting:** ✋ Pet eases the camera into a close side shot. Stroke your dog with the mouse or your finger, or hold Space / gamepad A. The dog leans into your hand, squints, wags faster the more you pet, and thumps a back leg when you rub its side. Hearts come off your hand. A few seconds of real petting earns Bond XP (server-checked).
- **Fetch:** 🎾 Ball equips a real ball tool. Hold to wind up (a dotted arc and landing ring show the throw; your dog crouches, ready), release to throw. The ball is real physics and bounces. Your dog waits a beat, sprints, grabs it (air catches get a banner), trots back proudly, drops it at your feet and sits for praise. The ball pops back into your hand. Roofs and out-of-bounds throws "poof" and you get a new ball.
- **Alive:** blinking, looking at you or the ball, head tilts, sniffing, scratching, play bows, little hops, yawns, lying down after a long rest, "Woof!" bubbles, nom-nom while eating.

**Missing production assets** (the code has placeholders and hooks): bark / whine / pant / crunch / squeaky-ball / bounce sounds (`Client/UI/Sounds.luau` `CUSTOM`), authored dog animations for sit, lie, eat, pet-lean, run, catch, and tricks (`PetCatalog.AnimationIds`), a hand cursor image and heart particle textures.

## Architecture

```
src/shared/   ReplicatedStorage.Shared       (client + server)
  Catalogs/   PetCatalog, ItemCatalog, QuestCatalog   ← data, no logic
  DogRig      builds the one procedural dog rig (no uploaded assets)
  Accessories turns an item's `visual` spec into welded parts
  Places      named areas (zones, waypoints)
  Remotes     the 2 network channels: Request (RemoteFunction), Push (RemoteEvent)

src/server/   ServerScriptService.Server
  Core/       Net (routing + rate limits), EventBus, ActionService
  Data/       ProfileSchema (versioned profile + migrations)
  Logic/      EncounterEngine, Conditions, Purchase   ← pure, unit-tested
  Content/    NPCs, Encounters, StoryArcs             ← writers/reviewers edit here
  Services/   PlayerData, Economy, Inventory, Pet, PetAppearance,
              Activity, Quest, NPC, Encounter, WorldBuilder

src/client/   StarterPlayerScripts.Client
  Controllers/ State, HUD, Adoption, Dialogue, Shop, Pet (animation), World (waypoint, fetch, blocked NPCs)
  UI/Theme    colors + UI helpers

tests/        ServerStorage.Tests
```

**How the pieces talk**

- **Client → server:** `Request:InvokeServer(kind, payload)`. Every kind is rate-limited and validated. The client only sends ids (item id, choice id); the server decides every outcome.
- **Server → client:** `Push` sends a fresh profile snapshot after every change, plus toasts, dialogue nodes, and so on.
- **Events** (`EventBus`): something happened. `pet.adopted`, `activity.completed`, `item.purchased`, `item.equipped`, `quest.completed`, `encounter.completed`. Quests and Jordan's story listen to these.
- **Actions** (`ActionService`): named commands content can trigger, such as `grant_currency`, `equip_item`, `unlock_activity`, `simulate_block`, `simulate_report`, `open_help`, `pet_react`. Unknown action names are flagged at startup and in tests.

**NPC encounters** (the core abstraction)

- `NPCDefinition` (Content/NPCs): who, where, role (`peer`, `trusted_adult`, `shopkeeper`), which arcs and encounters.
- `EncounterDefinition` (Content/Encounters): nodes of lines and choices; choices have conditions, actions, a next node, and an outcome. Includes curriculum metadata and `reviewStatus`.
- `StoryArcDefinition` (Content/StoryArcs): versioned, ordered stages with outcome-based transitions.
- `PlayerNPCProgress` (`profile.arcs[arcId]`, `profile.npcs[npcId]`): stage, completed encounters, decisions, flags, blocked/reported. **Per player**, so two players at the same Jordan see different dialogue.
- NPC models are tagged `Interactable_NPC` (attributes `NpcId`, `LocationId`) and get a `ProximityPrompt`. The server re-checks distance.

To add an encounter: add an entry to `Encounters.luau`, reference it from an NPC (`encounters`) or an arc stage, and run the tests. Content validation catches missing nodes, dead ends, and unknown actions or conditions.

## Safety design choices

- Block / Report / Get help appear in **every** conversation with a peer NPC (Riley too), like real chat tools. They aren't "the right answers" to a quiz.
- Agreeing to Jordan's request leads to a gentle chance to reconsider. It is never punished. Every path ends on a short, supportive Ranger Tip, and the player keeps all rewards.
- Practice Block/Report clearly say they don't contact Roblox, and explain the real Roblox tools.
- Help-seeking is never gated or rewarded. Learning data is minimal booleans (`profile.learning`); there is no score.
- The dog comforts and celebrates. It never "detects" anyone.
- No real-money purchases, no free-text chat with NPCs, no generated dialogue.

## Not done yet

- Persistence is implemented (session-locked `UpdateAsync`, autosave, `BindToClose`) but has **not been tested against a real DataStore**. That needs a published place.
- Multiplayer has not been tested with 2+ clients (Studio → Test → Clients and Servers).
- Mobile and gamepad layouts were built for but not checked on devices.
- Sounds use one built-in chime. There are no custom animations or art assets yet.
- P1 and later items: more breeds (add to `PetCatalog`), more arcs, multiple owned pets, renaming.
